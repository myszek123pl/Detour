## 1. What they SAY

### Cupsey (@Cupseyy)
- X post, July 2024: claimed >$1,000,000 made on pump.fun alone, "0 insider plays, 0 raydium coins", "in the trenches 12+ hours a day". Self-reported. Source: https://x.com/Cupseyy/status/1808348461739241721
- Interview summary (secondary, via a HuggingFace guide, unread directly): acknowledges he may have shifted Solana trading toward faster quick-flips; says today's meta is far more extreme than his original style; advice paraphrased as "spot crime and you sneak into the crime" (i.e. trade around manipulated/bundled launches rather than avoid them). The summary also lists losing holds (e.g. 3 SOL into a coin at ~8K mcap, 6 SOL at ~20K mcap), so he does not claim to avoid bags. Confidence: low-medium.
- Style per third-party sites (uwuu.ai, memecoin.wiki): early-minute entries, high frequency, fast exits, volume over conviction. uwuu.ai quotes ~78 trades/day, >90% winners, ~$191 avg profit/trade: commercial analytics, unverified. A KuCoin item claims only one red day in 7 months (~$400); unverified.
- Style change 2024 -> 2026: the only evolution evidence is his own remark that the meta became more extreme/faster than his original approach (low confidence).

### Ansem
- Older interviews: ~70% majors / ~30% speculative allocation; test each position by asking "would I buy this same size today?" if not, trim/exit. Memecoin selection: relatability / meme resonance with people outside crypto (Benzinga, 2024). Said at one point he "lost his advantage" and made poor decisions in a bad market (summary, unverified wording).
- Said there was a "misalignment of incentives" in certain celebrity-backed memecoins (The Block, ~Oct 2024; not read).
- Sizing/entry rules: not found.

### Cented
- Public positioning: ultra-short-term flipping. KuCoin flash ("7-Second Trader") reports >$10M profit over 308,666 trades in 357 days, trades support/resistance on obscure tokens within seconds; address flagged by some as an MEV-like bot; copy-trading him reportedly returned -21.3%. These are third-party stats (likely Kolscan/analytics), not his statements. Source: https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/cented-the-7-second-trader-sparks-debate-over-meme-coin-ecosystem

### Orangie (Gabriel Leyva)
- Content creator/streamer; says he shows wins and losses (e.g. a ~$13K live-stream loss); uses mobile trading apps (FOMO) and AI research tools per secondary summaries. Process rules not verified. Conflict of interest: income from audience/content and platform relationships.

### Murad
- Thesis-driven, not scalping: "Memecoins will be the biggest winners of this cycle"; holds large positions (SPX etc.); in 2026 reporting still holding despite large drawdown (Cointelegraph "memecoin messiah lost $60M... still not selling", per DropsTab; not read). Not a trenches flipper, so not comparable to Cupsey/Cented.

## 3. Conflicts of interest to weigh
- Self-reported PnL on X and in podcasts is unaudited and selective (survivorship).
- Callout rewards, platform deals (pump.fun/FOMO referral and creator rewards) and paid token promos create incentives to publicize coins they hold. Bubblemaps' Cupsey report cites callout-linked rewards; Sahil's claim concerns Ansem paid promos (denied).
- Speed scalpers (Cupsey, Cented) profit partly from copy-trader/audience liquidity and MEV-like latency edge that retail cannot replicate; third-party stats show copying fails (Cented -21.3%).
- Large holders (Murad) can move markets by posting; ZachXBT's front-running inference is contested.
- Analytics vendors (uwuu.ai etc.) and AI transcript sites are commercial/secondary.

## 4. Synthesis (tentative)
- Setups: two archetypes. (a) Speed/volume: first-minutes pump.fun entries, many small trades, fast exits, tolerate bundled/"crime" launches (Cupsey, Cented). (b) Thesis/narrative holder: relatable memes, larger sizes, long holds, tolerates deep drawdowns (Murad, partly Ansem).
- Sourcing: pump.fun feeds, 12+ hrs/day screen time (Cupsey claim), social/narrative for relatability (Ansem).
- Sizing/exits/risk: explicit numbers largely absent from verified sources; only anecdotes (3-6 SOL bets at 8-20K mcap; Ansem's "would I rebuy" test). Generic frameworks found (1% risk, 25% ladders at 2x/3x/5x/10x from Altrady/Trojan) are NOT attributable to these traders.
- Biggest mistakes: only Ansem's vague "lost my advantage"; Murad's unrealized $60M drawdown. Time-to-consistency: not found.
