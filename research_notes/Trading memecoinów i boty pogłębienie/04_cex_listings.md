## 1. Event-study evidence: returns before/on/after announcement, decay

### Takeaway
Announcement-day returns are strongly positive (Coinbase and Binance, per one working paper), with a pre-announcement run-up and a fade or flat/negative drift once trading starts. Magnitudes vary by window, benchmark and sample, and effects appear to have shrunk over time.

### Cited Findings
- A working paper reports significantly positive announcement-day returns for both Binance and Coinbase. The average first-day return is 32.8% for Coinbase and 22.7% for Binance. The authors attribute the gap to a "certification effect": Coinbase is more regulated and more selective. (Figures as relayed by search summary; paper not opened; it is likely the Loyola Sellinger conference paper by J. Li — unconfirmed.) — [J Li, Loyola](https://loyola.edu/_media/sellinger-business/documents/academics/departments/finance/wealth-management-conference/speakers/J_Li.pdf) (listed in search results; claims taken from summary of results)
- The TIE (2026 industry analysis, BTC-adjusted returns): once trading starts on the new venue, the adjusted move largely dissipates and goes flat or negative shortly after listing. Its conclusion is that the announcement matters more than the listing itself. It suggests Upbit's same-day or hours-ahead notices concentrate the reaction at the event. — [The TIE](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026)
- The TIE also has a piece on Binance listings for tokens already on 3+ major exchanges, which is relevant to the marginal effect of a further listing. Content not read. — [The TIE](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-a-binance-listing-delivers-for-already-listed-tokens)
- RockawayX describes a price increase in the week before listing followed by a gradual sell-off. — [RockawayX](https://rockawayx.com/insights/bullish-or-bearish-what-really-happens-when-tokens-list-on-a-cex)
- Older, non-comparable industry figures: Messari reported a 91% price jump in the first 5 days after Coinbase listings; Ren & Heinrich reported a 73% higher maximum price in the 30 days after Binance listings. Windows, benchmarks and samples differ from the academic estimates. (Primary sources not located; unverified.) — relayed in the search summary of [search results](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026)
- Other relevant items surfaced but not read: Yahoo headline "'Binance Effect' Means 41% Price Spike for Newly Listed Tokens" ([link](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/binance-effect-means-41-price-173526978.html)); Coin Metrics "Do Coinbase Listings Turn Altcoins Into Gold?" ([link](https://coinmetrics.io/?p=3693)); a 2024 exchange-listing-effect report ([link](https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/2524569282)); a Dagstuhl AFT 2025 paper ([link](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol354-aft2025/html/LIPIcs.AFT.2025.11/LIPIcs.AFT.2025.11.html)); an EUR thesis ([link](https://thesis.eur.nl/pub/59675/Final-Draft.pdf)). The 41% figure is a headline only; methodology unknown.

### Inferences
- The tradable edge is mostly in the pre-announcement and announcement window; buying at or after the listing start has historically been flat to negative (BTC-adjusted).
- Selective, regulated venues (Coinbase) likely carry a bigger certification premium than permissive ones, but memecoin-specific data is missing.

### Gaps
- No memecoin-only event study found. No Bybit, OKX, Robinhood or Upbit quantitative figures verified. No CAR windows (e.g. -10/+10 days) or sample sizes obtained. Need to open the J Li paper, the Dagstuhl AFT 2025 paper and the Coin Metrics report.

## 2. Pre-listing leaks / insider buying; roadmap vs actual listings

### Takeaway
Evidence of pre-announcement run-ups is consistent with information leakage or anticipation, but I could not verify specific cases, enforcement actions, or roadmap-to-listing conversion rates.

### Cited Findings
- Returns build before announcements in several sources; a pre-listing week run-up then sell-off is described. — [RockawayX](https://rockawayx.com/insights/bullish-or-bearish-what-really-happens-when-tokens-list-on-a-cex)
- Effects appear to have shrunk over time as pre-listing run-ups became more common (search summary's characterization; unverified). — [The TIE](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026)

### Inferences
- A run-up before announcement implies that waiting for the public announcement captures less than the headline averages suggest.

### Gaps
- Searches on insider trading/leak cases (Binance, Upbit, Coinbase employee cases), Coinbase roadmap conversion rates, and Robinhood listing reactions failed (rate limit). Unverified background only, no figures: Coinbase publishes a roadmap of assets under consideration; roadmap additions have historically produced short-lived pops. Needs follow-up.
