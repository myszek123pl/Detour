# Institutional / whale participation in memecoins (public entities; as of Nov 2026)

Method note: 4 web searches (budget ~5); search-snippet-level evidence only, no page fetches. Many figures are secondary (aggregators). Items marked [UNVERIFIED] lack a primary source or a date.

## 1. What do funds and market makers (DWF, Wintermute, GSR, Jump) publicly do in memecoins?

### Takeaway
DWF Labs and Wintermute have the most documented memecoin links (fund/market-making roles, Lookonchain-flagged wallets); Jump and GSR have no memecoin-specific reporting found. Most evidence is from 2024-2025, not 2026.

### Cited Findings
- DWF Labs launched a $20M fund dedicated to memecoins; DWF has described memecoins as having become "a sophisticated market vertical" — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/332873/memecoins-have-transitioned-into-a-sophisticated-market-vertical-dwf-labs); [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/markets/dwf-labs-rides-memecoins-rise-with-new-fund/)
- Lookonchain spotted a DWF-linked wallet moving $5M USDT to a Milady multisig signer — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/dwf-labs-to-invest-in-memecoins-onchain-data-reveals-ladys-transactions/)
- Lookonchain: "It seems that Wintermute has become market maker for ACT"; coverage frames Wintermute as pushing to overtake DWF in memecoin market making, with increased GOAT exposure — [Coin Edition](https://coinedition.com/wintermutes-meme-coin-dominance-taking-on-dwf-labs-with-goat/); [iTiger/ACT rumor](https://itiger.com/news/2483079012)
- Lookonchain traced ~$2.08M of LAPTOP sales to an Arkham-labelled Wintermute wallet (part of a team allocation; relationship undisclosed) — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/markets/report-links-2-08m-in-laptop-sales-to-wintermute-tagged-wallet)
- Wintermute/DWF dispute: Wintermute alleged DWF manipulation; DWF denied it — [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/9994a-dwf-labs-makes-massive-transaction-amidst-allegations-of-market-manipulation)
- Suspicion pieces about DWF deploying/manipulating memecoins exist but are a Substack, low authority — [WuBlock](https://wublock.substack.com/p/research-how-dwf-labs-is-suspected)
- Jump: Hyperliquid analysis describes a master account with 16 sub-accounts running high-volume taker/arbitrage; ~$300M+ ETH/stETH/stablecoins attributed on Ethereum (secondary source); Terra/UST lawsuit alleges manipulation, Jump disputes — [youmind analysis](https://youmind.com/landing/x-viral-articles/jump-trading-hyperliquid-wallet-analysis); [Insights4VC](https://insights4vc.substack.com/p/inside-jump-crypto-13b-terra-trade)
- GSR: invested in 100+ companies/protocols and provides liquidity on 60+ exchanges (Feb 2025); listed among top-4 market makers in 2026 — [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/vn-top-market-makers-altcoins-2026)

### Inferences
- Token-team allocations held by market makers (LAPTOP case) are a recurring pattern; MM wallet inflows to exchanges are plausible sell signals but intent is not proven.
- Jump and GSR appear mainly in majors/infrastructure, not memecoins, based on absence of evidence only.

### Gaps
- No 2026 reports found on DWF/Wintermute memecoin positions; no Jump/GSR memecoin reports found; no verified PnL for any of them.

## 2. Whale accumulation signals (Lookonchain / Spot On Chain) and how flows related to moves

### Takeaway
Lookonchain/Spot On Chain frequently flag fresh wallets buying memecoins, but the public record found shows mixed outcomes and mostly anecdotal, not systematic, evidence that flows preceded price moves.

### Cited Findings
- CASHCAT: Lookonchain flagged >$1.7M accumulation (new wallet ~13.14M tokens for 424 ETH; second wallet ~16M for $1M) as token rose ~27% in a day; token was ~61% below its $0.2288 ATH of 11 Jul 2026 — [CryptoBasic](https://thecryptobasic.com/flash-news/whales-buy-over-1-7m-in-cashcat-as-meme-coin-jumps-27/) (secondary; the bounce coincided with/followed the buying)
- WIF/POPCAT: wallet withdrew ~7.23-7.25M WIF from Binance and bought POPCAT (~5.77M); total ~$13.34M-$18.57M across 4 memecoins; wallet was ~22 days old; date unconfirmed — [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/5bf22-whale-purchases-18-57m-worth-of-4-memecoins) [UNVERIFIED date]
- TRUMP: whales kept buying while token ~90% below peak, yet weak retail demand and heavy futures outflows ($547.9M out vs $504M in over 7 days) held price down — [AMBCrypto](https://ambcrypto.com/trump-memecoin-slides-90-from-ath-but-why-are-whales-still-loading-up/) (whale buying did not stop the decline)
- Spot On Chain: whale bought 495K TRUMP with 250K USDT on Jan 19 and later partly sold for profit — [Spot On Chain Telegram](https://t.me/s/spotonchain/863)
- PEPE Jan 2026: whale sold 129B PEPE at ~$151k loss — [AMBCrypto roundup](https://ambcrypto.com/memecoins-explode-as-2026-begins-but-the-first-cracks-are-showing/)
- Earlier example: largest WIF holder aggressive accumulation (Aug 2024) and whales taking profit as WIF erased ~20% of May 2024 gains — [Daily Hodl](https://dailyhodl.com/2024/08/26/largest-dogwifhat-wif-holder-continues-aggressive-accumulation-of-solanas-top-memecoin-lookonchain); [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/whales-take-profit-on-wif-holdings-meme-coin-wipes-out-nearly-20-gains-from-may-top-202405230857)
- Fartcoin: "smart trader" added another $536K USDT trade (Apr 2025) — [Daily Hodl](https://dailyhodl.com/2025/04/29/smart-trader-doubles-down-on-memecoin-investment-places-another-536000-usdt-trade-on-fartcoin-lookonchain)
- Wintermute BTC flows (not memecoins): $107M BTC to Binance on NYE read as distribution; Oct 2026 40 new wallets withdrew 9,458 BTC (Lookonchain: accumulation, recipients unidentified) — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/?p=513493); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/bitcoin-whales-780m-wintermute-withdrawals/)
- One report says whale-activity indicator jumps coincided with PEPE/FLOKI surges — [MEXC News](https://www.mexc.com/news/438484) (aggregator, weak)

### Inferences
- Reports are survivorship-biased: Lookonchain posts highlight notable wallets, often after price moves have begun, so lead time is unclear.
- Whale accumulation in deep drawdown tokens (TRUMP, CASHCAT) is not a reliable bottom signal.
- Exchange withdrawals by fresh wallets are the commonly flagged pattern; no systematic hit-rate data found.

### Gaps
- No documented case found linking a Wintermute-funded wallet to a memecoin accumulate-then-dump sequence.
- No quantitative study of lead time or hit rate of Lookonchain/Spot On Chain signals; primary X posts were not accessed.
- Most 2026 items are secondary summaries; exact dates and amounts should be re-checked before publication.
