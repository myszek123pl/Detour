## Bot edge (less competition) vs risks (low liquidity, bridges)

### Takeaway
Not verified from sources. Reasoning only: smaller chains likely mean fewer sniper bots but thin liquidity, exit risk, bridge risk and lower volumes (consistent with sector-wide volume decline above).

### Cited Findings
- Launchpad volume overall down ~69% from Sept 2026 peak, i.e. thin conditions broadly — [OneBullEx](https://www.onebullex.com/news/articles/meme-coin-launchpad-volume-slides-69-to-550-million-daily)
- SunPump's small revenue suggests small fee pool/volume on Tron — [Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/best-crypto-launchpad-platforms)

### Inferences
- Edge is plausible only where a chain has a launchpad with real daily volume; given the revenue concentration at Pump.fun (~98%), absolute profit pool on other chains is probably small, so competition may be low because opportunity is low.
- Main risks: slippage and inability to exit, rug/honeypot prevalence, bridge/custody risk when moving capital, immature bot tooling and RPC/MEV infrastructure. These are inferences, not sourced.
