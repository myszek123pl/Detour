## PUMP raise size, valuation, buybacks, performance through 2026

### Takeaway
PUMP ICO (12 Jul 2025) sold out in ~12 minutes at $0.004 (~$4B FDV); raise is $500M on-chain vs $600M per the co-founder. Pump.fun spent ~100% of revenue on buybacks (~$350M by Apr 2026), then burned ~$370M of PUMP (~36% of circulating supply) in Apr 2026 and cut to 50% of net revenue for one year. Token traded sideways/well below peak through 2026 despite buybacks.

## Points/airdrop programs of trading platforms: volume inflation, wash trading, bot farming value

### Takeaway
No reliable evidence found of wash trading specifically on Axiom/GMGN/Photon; no confirmed Axiom or GMGN token as of search results. Axiom's program is fee rebates/referrals plus points; most "airdrop guides" are affiliate SEO. Farming value for a bot is likely small and speculative; precedent (Aevo) shows volume-reward programs attract wash volume.

### Cited Findings
- Axiom: team has not confirmed a token or airdrop campaign — [CoinGecko Learn](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/what-is-axiom-exchange)
- Axiom relies on fee rebates (up to 30% back in SOL), referral ladder, and Axiom Points — [DropsTab](https://dropstab.com/coins/axiom-exchange)
- Koinly (updated May 2026) lists Axiom as a rumored airdrop, describing cashback rewards and points tracking usage — [Koinly](https://koinly.io/blog/upcoming-crypto-airdrops/)
- Axiom earlier reported founders had no plans for a token (older article, may be stale); Axiom revenue reported at ~$150M, overtaking Cursor — [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/defi/solana-bot-axiom-takes-over-cursor-with-150m-revenue/)
- Axiom is a Y Combinator-backed terminal with ~$10M weekly revenue at one point — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/axiom-ycombinator-startup-solana-memecoin-revenue-10m)
- GMGN: no confirmed token/airdrop; guides speculate a snapshot would weigh volume, fees paid, referrals and exclude wash patterns (speculative, farming-guide claim) — [airdrops.io GMGN](https://airdrops.io/gmgn/)
- MEXC blog (promotional): rewards system updated for 2026 with an in-app notice in Dec 2025 to claim rewards before year-end — [MEXC](https://blog.mexc.com/token-reviews/axiom-trade-airdrop-2026-how-to-farm-points-on-solanas-highest-revenue-trading-terminal/)
- Precedent: Aevo 2024 volume spike alleged to be wash trading driven by volume-rewarding airdrop points; Aevo said it may be airdrop farming — [CoinDesk via Yahoo](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/billion-dollar-volumes-then-steep-175536429.html)
- Industry commentary argues volume farming destroys value — [Chainscore](https://www.chainscorelabs.com/blog/airdrop-strategies-and-community-building/airdrop-analytics-and-measurement/the-cost-of-cheap-engagement-why-volume-farming-destroys-value) (low-authority blog)

### Inferences
- Because trading terminals earn ~1% fees per trade, pure wash farming costs real fees; unlike perp DEXs with maker rebates, round-trip volume farming is costly unless points value exceeds ~1% of volume. Fee-rebate structures (cash back) make net cost lower but reduce wash incentive [inference].
- For a bot: points are an uncertain bonus; the rational approach is to trade for edge and treat points/rebates as a small side benefit, not the strategy. Sybil filtering is likely [inference].
- Pump.fun's PUMP airdrop/buyback precedent shows token launches by platforms can underperform, lowering expected airdrop value.
