## UK: FCA and the pump.fun block

### Takeaway
In Dec 2024 the FCA warned that pump.fun is unauthorised and may be targeting UK people; pump.fun then blocked UK users itself (voluntary geoblock, not a regulator order). Current (2026) status not verified.

### Cited Findings
- FCA warning published 3 Dec 2024: pump.fun not authorised, "may be targeting people in the UK"; no access to Financial Ombudsman Service or FSCS — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/329804/uk-top-financial-regulator-says-pump-fun-doesnt-have-its-permission-to-do-business-in-the-country); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/fca-issues-warning-bans-pump-fun-uk)
- Pump.fun updated its terms to block UK users days after the warning; Decrypt reports the platform was not forced to by the FCA — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/295355/solana-pump-fun-bans-uk-users-regulator); [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/pump-fun-updates-terms-to-block-uk-users-days-after-fca-warning/)
- Blockworks source argued it is not a ban per se; platform entity noted as Baton Corporation Ltd. — [Blockworks](https://www.blockworks.com/news/pumpdotfun-restricts-uk-access)

### Inferences
- Pattern: regulator warning (financial promotions regime) -> platform self-geoblocks. Expect the same template for other launchpads/bots marketing to UK users.

## US: SEC, CLARITY, GENIUS

### Takeaway
SEC staff (Feb 2025) said typical memecoins are not securities; fraud remains enforceable. CLARITY/GENIUS status in 2026 could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- SEC Corp Fin staff statement 27 Feb 2025: "typical" meme coins are not securities; non-binding; does not cover fraud — [SEC](https://sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/staff-statement-meme-coins); [Harvard Law CorpGov](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2025/03/19/implications-of-the-secs-stance-that-meme-coins-are-not-securities/)
- Commissioner Crenshaw dissented; some firms argue memecoins are commodities under the CEA, leaving CFTC/fraud jurisdiction open — [WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/sitecore/content/whlaunch/home/research/news-publications/20250313-the-state-of-meme-coin-regulation-sec-staffs-statement-and-other-considerations); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/03/03/meme-coins-sec)

### Inferences
- Memecoin trading in the US is largely outside securities law; risk shifts to fraud/anti-manipulation, CFTC, state law, tax.

### Gaps (UNVERIFIED training knowledge, no source)
- GENIUS Act (stablecoins) signed July 2025; implementing rules in 2026 not checked. Relevant to memecoin traders mainly via stablecoin on/off-ramps.
- CLARITY Act passed House July 2025; Senate status Nov 2026 unknown; memecoin/"digital commodity" classification and officials' memecoin ethics provisions were disputed.
