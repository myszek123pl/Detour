## Mechanics and creator fee sharing

### Takeaway
Creator-fee launchpads pay token creators a share of trading fees (Believe: 2% swap fee split 50/50 with platform; Bags: creator/fee-share on Meteora DBC then DAMM v2 pools). Details for Jupiter Studio, Moonshot, Heaven, Boop were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Believe launched early May 2025; fee structure is 2% buy/sell tax split evenly between Believe and the token creator; over $13M total fees reported by 14 May 2025 (as quoted in search snippet) — [The Block / Believe ecosystem](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2025-05-19-jito-validator-tips-hit-3-6-million-daily-as-believe-ecosystem-siphons-activity-from-pump-fun-354859); the same snippet says many Believe tokens had already dropped >70%.
- Bags: DeFiLlama counts fees paid swapping against Bags DBC pools (pre-migration) and DAMM v2 pools (post-migration); cumulative fees about $64.8M, about 99% Solana (snippet, date of snapshot unknown) — [DefiLlama Bags](https://defillama.com/protocol/bags)
- So Bags is built on Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve + DAMM v2 (inference from DefiLlama description above).
- Bags reached about $1B 30-day trading volume by Aug 2025; cumulative creator earnings about $21M at that time — [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/pump-fun-rival-bags-secures-1bn-user-trading-volume)
- Bags reportedly expanded to Robinhood Chain (single snippet, unverified) — [RootData](https://www.rootdata.com/projects/detail/bags?k=MTExOTI%3D)
- Bags token-launch activity surged after GAS token rose about 700% (headline only; details not read) — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/bags-launchpad-activity-surges-after-gas-token-soars-700)

### Inferences
- Jupiter Studio is widely understood to use Meteora DBC (from model prior knowledge, NOT verified in this session); OKX lists "JUP Studio" among smaller launchpads.

## Market share 2025-2026 and notable tokens

### Takeaway
Pump.fun remains dominant (about 67-74% in Aug 2025, about 70% cited early 2026) with brief LetsBonk flips; Bags is the strongest creator-fee challenger; share figures vary widely by source and window.

### Cited Findings
- LetsBonk briefly overtook Pump.fun in July 2025 (58.5% share on one day) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/328822/bonk-meme-coin-launchpad-flips-pump-fun-for-solana-tokens-created)
- Pump.fun regained about 73-74% of launchpad activity over 7 days by mid-Aug 2025 — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/how-pump-fun-captured-80-of-solana-memecoins-and-can-it-last); one report claims Pump went from about 5% to 90% of graduated launches within about two weeks — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/367266/solana-memecoin-launchpad-war-flips-again-as-pump-takes-top-spot-amid-letsbonk-collapse)
- Mid-Aug 2025 DeFiLlama-based analysis: Pump.fun about 67.9% and Bags about 11.6% (7-day, per search-engine summary of a secondary source; unverified)
- Undated Jupiter launchpad leaderboard snapshot: Pump 51.2% ($448M) vs Bags about 33.5% ($293M) for 1 day (summary; single-day, likely not comparable; unverified)
- Early 2026: Pump.fun about 70%; Bags credited with a 2026 breakthrough via mobile optimization and creator royalties, no figure — [KuCoin News](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/solana-ecosystem-rebound-bags-app-token-creation-hits-periodic-high-can-the-creator-economy-reshape-the-market)
- OKX: Pump.fun + LetsBonk about 90% of the market, with BAGS, JUP Studio, Believe the remainder — [OKX](https://www.okx.com/en-ae/learn/launchpad-wars-data-insights)
- Believe: Jito validator tips hit $3.6M/day in May 2025 as Believe activity grew — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2025-05-19-jito-validator-tips-hit-3-6-million-daily-as-believe-ecosystem-siphons-activity-from-pump-fun-354859)

## Effect of creator-fee incentives on dev behavior (rugging, launch counts)

### Takeaway
No rigorous evidence found. Only anecdotal: Believe tokens largely fell >70% soon after launch; sources note fraud/collapse risk persists on these platforms.

### Cited Findings
- Many Believe tokens down >70% within days of launch — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2025-05-19-jito-validator-tips-hit-3-6-million-daily-as-believe-ecosystem-siphons-activity-from-pump-fun-354859)
- Search summary for Bags noted reports that many launched tokens are fraudulent or collapse (secondary summary, source unspecified; unverified)

### Inferences
- Ongoing fee streams plausibly shift incentives from dump-once to volume-farming (more launches, churn, wash-like trading), but this is theory, not shown by data here. Fee-on-volume can also reward creators for hype without long-term value.
