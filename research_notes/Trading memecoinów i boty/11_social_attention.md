# Social media, attention and narrative as drivers of memecoin prices (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: these notes come from about 15 web searches. Full-text fetches of arxiv.org and sciencedirect.com failed because those hosts are blocked by this session's network policy. Findings marked "(via search summary)" were taken from search-result snippets and abstracts, not from the full papers, so check exact numbers against the originals before quoting them as headline figures. A lot of memecoin "research" online is promotional (presale ads, vendors selling volume or boosts). That material is flagged where it appears.

## 1. Empirical evidence: tweet volume, sentiment, Google Trends, mentions vs. memecoin price and volume (lead-lag)

### Takeaway
The evidence is consistent on one point. Social attention moves with memecoin returns and volume at the same time and feeds back on them ("attention-feedback trading"). It does not reliably predict returns days ahead. Where a lead exists, it is very short: minutes to hours for influencer posts, and up to a few days for search attention. Large-cap crypto studies often find that high attention predicts *lower* later returns, a reversal. The cleanest causal-looking effects come from single influencer events (Musk/DOGE), and those fade within hours.

### Cited Findings
- **Memecoin-specific attention-feedback trading:** A 2026 Research in International Business and Finance-type ScienceDirect paper, "Community, attention, and feedback trading in cryptocurrency markets: Evidence from memecoins", reports that memecoin turnover responds significantly to lagged absolute returns. The authors read this as attention-feedback trading: price moves draw attention, which draws volume. (Via search summary; the full text could not be fetched.) — [ScienceDirect S0275531926003491](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0275531926003491)
- **Twitter attention predicts reversal in the cross-section:** Pugachyov (FoFI 2024 conference paper, "Twitter-Based Attention and the Cross-Section of Cryptocurrency Returns") finds attention is positively related to *contemporaneous* returns and *negatively* predicts subsequent returns. — [Pugachyov, FoFI 2024](http://wp.lancs.ac.uk/fofi2024/files/2024/04/FoFI-2024-148-Nikolay-Pugachyov.pdf)
- **Musk/DOGE event study (Ante, Blockchain Research Lab):** Across 47 crypto-related Musk Twitter events, the study finds significant positive abnormal returns and abnormal trading volume. On average the price effects were significant only for Dogecoin, not Bitcoin. The "One word: Doge" tweet produced CAR of 8.17% within 5 minutes, peaking at 17.31% within 1 hour. Afterwards abnormal returns were much lower and no longer significant. — [BRL working paper](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/BRL-Working-Paper-No-16-How-Elon-Musks-Twitter-activity-moves-cryptocurrency-markets-v2.pdf); [BRL blog summary](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/2022/01/13/the-musk-effect-new-research-on-the-impact-of-elon-musks-twitter-activity-on-cryptocurrency-markets/); [Decrypt coverage](https://decrypt.co/56598/study-shows-elon-musk-influences-bitcoin-and-dogecoin-prices)
- **Counterpoint on direction of causality:** University of Kent work (Kheireddine & Adeyemi) argues Musk's tweets were, on average, a *reaction* to abnormal returns rather than a catalyst for them. — [Kent paper](https://media.www.kent.ac.uk/se/29936/KheireddineAdeyemiCryptocurrancyimpactsofTweeting.pdf); [Kent blog](https://blogs.kent.ac.uk/economics/2022/04/29/the-musk-effect/)
- **Google Trends:** A study in the International Journal of Emerging Markets (2022), using nonparametric causality-in-quantiles, finds two-way information flow between Google Trends attention and crypto returns at lags of up to about 6 days. (Via search summary.) — [ResearchGate: Google Trends and cryptocurrencies](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/360358876_Google_Trends_and_cryptocurrencies_a_nonparametric_causality-in-quantiles_analysis)
- **TikTok + Twitter for DOGE:** A 2025 arXiv preprint on multimodal sentiment reports that TikTok signals alone improved short-term Dogecoin predictions by about 35%, and that combining TikTok and Twitter sentiment improved forecasts further. This is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed. (Via search summary.) — [arXiv 2508.15825](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15825v1)
- **Mixed evidence on sentiment vs. volume:** One Erasmus thesis finds Twitter sentiment does not affect daily crypto returns, while Reddit sentiment does. Other work finds tweet *volume* a better predictor of price direction than tweet *sentiment*. — [Erasmus thesis (Hurjui)](https://thesis.eur.nl/pub/61895/Hurjui-R-466956.pdf); [arXiv 2307.15956 (tweet sentiment + user metadata, incl. DOGE/SHIB)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.15956)
- **Meme-stock analogue (useful as a methods caution):** An arXiv 2026 paper (2607.24072) on meme stocks finds lexicon (VADER) sentiment correlations with returns near zero within ±5 days. An LLM-based composite showed a strong same-day correlation followed by a reversal the next day (AMC). The authors conclude there is no uniformly stable predictive relationship across assets. — [arXiv 2607.24072](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24072v1)
- **Fragility framework:** "Measuring Memecoin Fragility" (arXiv 2512.00377) includes a "sentiment amplification" component that measures how attention-driven shocks affect memecoin market stability. (Via search summary.) — [arXiv 2512.00377](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00377v1)
- **Santiment social dominance as a euphoria/top gauge:** Santiment's social-dominance spikes for PEPE peaked just before declines, and Santiment reads high dominance as euphoria that has historically come before tops. Santiment also says memecoin-dominated attention cycles tend to come before broader corrections. This is anecdotal and chart-based, not a backtest. — [NewsBTC on PEPE / Santiment](https://www.newsbtc.com/news/pepe-coin-ready-another-pump-on-chain-data/amp/); [Cointelegraph / Santiment](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-top-altcoins-regaining-attention-heathier-market-cycle-santiment)
- **Santiment contrarian read on fading attention:** Santiment interpreted collapsing memecoin crowd interest and "end of meme era" talk as a capitulation-type setup, while noting that a rebound is not guaranteed. — [GNcrypto / Santiment](https://www.gncrypto.news/news/memecoin-hype-fades-as-market-cap-drops-sharply/)
- **Launch timing vs. social posts:** A 2026 study (reported as appearing at an ACM venue) of 15.2M Pump.fun tokens launched between Jan 2024 and Jan 2026 found about 23.5% were created shortly after posts on X or Truth Social. Timing alone does not show causation. (Secondary report.) — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/cryptocurrency-analytics-insight/how-social-media-influences-memecoin-market-activity)
- **Unverifiable correlations to avoid:** One "Best Meme Coins 2026" page quotes Santiment correlations of 0.73 and 0.68 with no traceable source. They should not be used. — [The Ledger Mind](https://theledgermind.com/best-meme-coins/)

### Inferences
- In practice, the usable "signal" is the *rate of change* of mentions or attention very early (minutes to hours), at the same time as on-chain flow, not daily sentiment. By the time daily attention or social dominance is high, the cross-sectional evidence (Pugachyov) and Santiment's own framing point to lower forward returns. Extreme attention works better as a contrarian or exit signal than as an entry signal.
- Volume and mention counts look more robust than sentiment polarity. Memecoin text is ironic, slang-heavy and bot-polluted, and that hurts lexicon sentiment in particular.
- Influencer-event effects exist but decay within about 1 hour (Musk/DOGE). Capturing them takes low-latency monitoring and execution, so it is a race against bots.
- Reverse causality (price → posts) is at least as strong as posts → price. Any backtest needs Granger or lead-lag tests in both directions and has to control for lagged returns and volume.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed, memecoin-specific, intraday lead-lag study (e.g., Granger tests of X mention velocity vs. Pump.fun/Raydium flow) with public results. The ScienceDirect memecoin paper is the closest, and I could not access its full text, lag structure or effect sizes.
- I found no independent backtest of LunarCrush Galaxy Score/AltRank, Santiment social dominance, or Kaito mindshare as memecoin return predictors.
- TikTok evidence is limited to one preprint on DOGE. I found nothing on small-cap Solana/Base memecoins.

## 2. Narrative cycles (AI agents, political, animals, celebrities, cult coins, CTOs) and rotation speed

### Takeaway
Memecoin capital rotates between themes (animals/dogs → AI agents → political/celebrity → "cult" coins, with CTO revivals throughout). Practitioners describe rotations lasting days to about a week at the sub-sector level. I found no rigorous measurement of rotation speed. Political and celebrity coins are tightly tied to news cycles and had weakened by mid/late 2026. The 2025-26 memecoin market as a whole was weaker than the 2024 peak.

### Cited Findings
- **Rotation framing:** One analytics vendor describes rotation as capital and attention moving between themes: dog coins may lead for a week, then rotation into AI memes, then political tokens. This is a vendor's framing, not a measured result. — [Sharpe.ai memecoin narrative terminal](https://www.sharpe.ai/products/memecoins)
- **AI-agent narrative:** KuCoin's Q2 2026 outlook frames AI-agent memecoins (e.g., GOAT) and "AI meme indices" as a theme. Tokenminds claims AI/agent narratives took over 62% of investor interest in Q1. I could not verify that figure, and the source is promotional. — [KuCoin Q2 2026](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/q2-2026-memecoin-outlook-top-10); [Tokenminds](https://tokenminds.co/blog/crypto-narratives)
- **Political (PolitiFi):** Tangem (Apr 2026) says PolitiFi tokens drew renewed attention ahead of the 2026 US midterms, and describes TRUMP as a token whose price tracks news cycles, media coverage and political events. An Aug 2026 item notes that political memecoin weakness had pushed discussion toward utility platforms rather than news-cycle tokens. — [Tangem, Apr 2026](https://tangem.com/en/blog/post/top-meme-coins/); [StreetInsider (press release, promotional)](https://www.streetinsider.com/MarketMediaWire/Memecoin+News+2026:+MemeToro+Publishes+AI+Agent+Architecture+As+BNB+Chain+Tokens+Rally/26949812.html)
- **Cult coins:** A Medium/Coinmonks essay names SPX6900 and GIGA as mid-caps with cult followings that held through 70% drawdowns. This is a single opinion source. The same essay describes Nov 2025 as bearish, with memecoin volume down and launches failing. — [Coinmonks](https://medium.com/coinmonks/everyone-thinks-memecoins-are-dead-2026-will-prove-them-catastrophically-wrong-71ca98644f6a)
- **Attention shifting away from memes:** Santiment reported BTC and top altcoins taking social attention back from memecoins and called that a healthier cycle. — [Cointelegraph / Santiment](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-top-altcoins-regaining-attention-heathier-market-cycle-santiment)
- **CTO (community takeover) mechanics:** A CTO happens when the dev abandons or dumps a token and holders take over its socials (new X and Telegram accounts, a website) and claim the DEX Screener profile to update the logo, description and links. Paid DEX Screener boosts are commonly read as a sign of commitment. — [DEXTools CTO guide 2026](https://www.dextools.io/tutorials/what-is-a-cto-in-crypto-guide-2026); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/what-is-a-community-takeover-cto-when-a-memecoins-holders-seize-the-wheel/); [CoinCodex](https://coincodex.com/article/63468/cto-crypto-meaning)
- **CTO success story and risks:** Decrypt covered BILLY (Solana) surviving abandonment by its dev through a CTO. Other Decrypt reporting covers legal exposure for takeover teams. Guides warn that CTOs are often a setup for a rug, that low-effort attempts far outnumber genuine revivals, and that some "CTO" teams are run by the original deployer. — [Decrypt: BILLY](https://decrypt.co/241846/how-solana-meme-coin-billy-survived-being-abandoned-by-its-dev); [Decrypt: legal risk](https://decrypt.co/247781/meme-coin-takeover-teams-face-legal-entanglement-experts-say); [MadeOnSol CTO guide](https://madeonsol.com/blog/solana-community-takeovers-cto-radar-guide)
- **Platform formalising CTOs:** The Pump.fun founder teased a community-takeover feature tied to revenue sharing. DEX Screener shows "A community claimed ownership for this token" banners on token pages (example dated Apr 25, 2026). — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/pumpfun-founder-teases-community-takeover-feature-for-revenue-sharing); [DEX Screener example](https://dexscreener.com/solana/8WPerPpfuYx8sPddgk5eZL3DJygRNhgdbTATvxZZRneb)

### Inferences
- Rotation speed scales with market regime. In 2024's peak, sub-narratives turned over in days. In the weaker 2025-26 market, fewer narratives catch on and most die within hours of launch.
- Political and celebrity tokens are event-driven (news-cycle betas) and are better modelled as reactions to news than as standalone trends.
- A CTO is mainly a *social* re-launch signal: new socials plus a DEX Screener profile claim plus boosts. That makes it machine-detectable (profile-claim events and boost purchases), but it also has a high base rate of rugs.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative study measuring how long memecoin narratives last (e.g., half-life of sector mindshare) or the order in which they rotate. All rotation claims come from vendors or practitioners.
- No data on CTO success rates.
- The Q1 2026 "62% AI-agent share" figure is unverified.

## 3. Data sources and APIs for social signals; reliability; bots and fake engagement

### Takeaway
X API access is now pay-per-use and expensive at scale. Since Jan 2026, X has also cut off apps that pay users to post, which killed "InfoFi" yap-to-earn schemes (Kaito Yaps, Cookie Snaps). LunarCrush sells aggregated social metrics from $90/mo or $1 per 1,000 requests. Santiment and Kaito provide curated metrics. The biggest problem is data quality: bots, paid KOLs, comment bots and Telegram call groups create a large share of visible "attention", and returns from following call channels are negative at the median.

### Cited Findings
- **X API pricing:** X made pay-per-use (credit-based, no base subscription) the default for new developers on Feb 6, 2026, and ended the free tier for new signups. Legacy Basic ($200/mo) and Pro ($5,000/mo) remain only for existing subscribers. One source says those subscribers moved to pay-per-use after June 1, 2026 (unconfirmed). Secondary guides cite about $0.005 per post read, and $0.01 per user lookup or post create, but guides disagree. Monthly read caps are reported as 2M or 3M. Enterprise pricing is unpublished. The official rate card is at docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing. — [Open Terms Archive memo](https://opentermsarchive.org/en/memos/x-replaces-its-dollar200-entry-level-api-plan-with-a-pay-per-use-model/); [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260209-x-api-pay-per-use); [Roboin](https://roboin.io/article/en/2026/02/08/x-transitions-api-to-pay-per-use-model-ending-free-plan/); [Postproxy](https://postproxy.dev/blog/x-api-pricing-2026); [Blotato](https://www.blotato.com/blog/twitter-api-pricing); [OpenTweet](https://opentweet.io/x-api-pricing)
- **X bans InfoFi (Jan 15, 2026):** X revoked API access for apps that reward users for posting, citing "AI slop and reply spam" (product lead Nikita Bier). Kaito shut down Yaps and its incentivised Yapper Leaderboards and replaced them with Kaito Studio. Kaito Pro, its API and its Capital Launchpad kept running. KAITO fell about 15-20% (about $0.70 → $0.55). Cookie shut down Snaps. — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/x-kills-kaito-infofi-tokens-crash/); [Protos](https://protos.com/nikita-bier-causes-collapse-of-crypto-infofi/); [Crypto Economy](https://crypto-economy.com/x-bans-infofi-apps-forcing-kaito-to-sunset-yaps-and-pivot-strategy/); [Coin Edition](https://coinedition.com/x-shuts-down-infofi-apps-triggering-kaito-token-sell-off-and-scrutiny/)
- **Kaito after the ban:** Kaito partnered with Polymarket on "attention markets" (Feb 2026). A July 2026 report says Kaito gained official access to X's data firehose. — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/kaito-ai-data-agreement-x/); [Web3Wagmi Kaito review](https://blog.web3wagmi.com/kaito-review)
- **LunarCrush:** Plans start at $90/mo with API and MCP access. Medium is $300/mo and Heavy $900/mo, each 20% less billed annually. Enterprise is custom. Pay-as-you-go is $1 per 1,000 requests after 1,000 free credits. The free tier is extremely limited, and commercial use requires a paid plan. Metrics include Galaxy Score, AltRank, social volume, sentiment and social dominance across coins, stocks and 50+ topic categories. — [LunarCrush pricing](https://lunarcrush.com/pricing); [LunarCrush API docs (GitHub v4)](https://github.com/lunarcrush/api)
- **Santiment:** Santiment provides social volume, social dominance and crowd-sentiment metrics, used in press analysis of PEPE, WIF and other memecoins. Press articles conflict on PEPE vs. WIF social-volume rankings depending on the period. — [AMBCrypto PEPE vs WIF](https://eng.ambcrypto.com/pepe-vs-wif-analyzing-social-sentiment-trading-volume-trends-post-correction/); [AMBCrypto PEPE buzz](https://ambcrypto.com/as-pepes-social-buzz-fades-whats-next-for-price/)
- **Comment bots on Pump.fun:** "Meme Coin Factories" (arXiv 2609.10246) studied about 15M Pump.fun coins over two years. It documents manipulators deploying automated bots that post comments on coin pages, and confirms 14 repositories advertising Pump.fun comment bots. It cites Ding et al. (2025) on comment bots and price changes. (Via search summary.) — [arXiv 2609.10246](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10246v1)
- **Bot share of volume:** A Dune dashboard by Naveen_0505 claims bots account for 60-80% of turnover in some memecoins. This is single-analyst monitoring and is not peer reviewed. — [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/expert-highlights-bot-infiltration-on-pump-funs-meme-token-platform/)
- **Telegram call channels:** A Substack analysis of a Jan-Mar 2025 Telegram export (144,913 messages, 8,118 signal messages) found the idealized average return of +552.2% hid a median of -4.0%. Bot competition erodes what retail followers can capture. — [Money Leaves Clues (Substack)](https://moneyleavesclues.substack.com/p/inside-the-economics-of-pumpfun-call)
- **Pump-and-dump base rates:** Solidus Labs (vendor) reports about 98.7% of Pump.fun tokens showed pump-and-dump or rug characteristics. Chainalysis flagged 74,037 tokens launched in 2024 with patterns potentially consistent with pump-and-dumps. — [Solidus Labs report](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- **KOL wallet identification:** An arXiv paper on resisting manipulative bots in memecoin copy-trading reports a multi-agent LLM system that identifies high-quality memecoin projects and KOL wallets at 73% and 70% precision. The selected KOLs generated about $500k in total profit. — [arXiv 2601.08641](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08641v1)
- **Multimodal datasets:** MemeChain (cross-chain multimodal memecoin forensics dataset) and CoinCLIP (multimodal viability scoring from token images and text) show that academic signals are moving toward image, text and on-chain fusion. — [arXiv 2601.22185](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.22185); [arXiv 2412.07591](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.07591)

### Inferences
- For a trading bot, raw X data is costly. Reading 1M posts/month is about $5k at the commonly cited $0.005/read, if that rate holds. That pushes most builders toward aggregators (LunarCrush, Santiment, Kaito API), third-party X scrapers, or tracking a curated list of KOL and alpha accounts instead of firehose-wide counts.
- Since attention can be bought or faked (comment bots, Telegram calls, paid KOLs, and InfoFi before 2026), weighting matters. Weighting by account quality (age, follower authenticity, past hit-rate) and checking against on-chain unique buyers and wallet clustering is needed before treating mentions as organic.
- The X InfoFi ban probably cut one source of manufactured crypto engagement in 2026, but organic and engineered memecoin shilling on X continues. It also means "mindshare" leaderboards from before 2026 are not comparable with those after.
- Telegram call-channel signals carry negative expected value for followers at the median. Their main use for a bot is as a *flow* predictor, since followers will buy, which can only be exploited by front-running, and that is ethically and legally fraught.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the exact current X pay-per-use rates or caps from docs.x.com. Secondary sources conflict ($0.005 vs. other figures; 2M vs. 3M cap).
- I found no Santiment pricing data, and no independent accuracy audits of LunarCrush, Santiment or Kaito sentiment classifiers.
- I found no rigorous estimate of what share of memecoin X engagement is bot-generated. The 60-80% figure is for trading volume, not social engagement.
- I found no research on Telegram scraping practices (MTProto/Telethon), group coverage, or reliability.

## 4. DEX Screener trending, boosts and paid ads: effect on flows

### Takeaway
DEX Screener Boosts are paid multipliers on a token's existing Trending Score, cost about $99-$3,999, and do not guarantee a top spot. The only analytical evidence found (Dethective, via a secondary outlet) suggests boosted tokens often dump during or after the boost, and that the platform, launchpads, bots and KOLs profit at retail's expense. I found no independent causal study.

### Cited Findings
- **Mechanics (official):** DEX Screener says Boosts temporarily raise a token's Trending Score by applying a multiplier to the existing score, to increase visibility. They do not guarantee a top position. — [DEX Screener docs: Boosting](https://docs.dexscreener.com/boosting)
- **Pricing:** Boosts range from about $99 to $3,999, with the top tier ($3,999) cited for 24 hours. Token Ads are a separate product from Boosts. — [Medium / ListDesk on DEX Screener ad costs](https://medium.com/@listdesk/how-much-do-dexscreener-ads-cost-what-each-option-really-buys-cc4325d964bc); [OpenLiquid: Is boost worth it in 2026](https://openliquid.io/blog/dexscreener-boost-feature-explained/)
- **Boosts ≠ trending list:** The API exposes boosted tokens separately from organic trending, and boosts do not create liquidity, broader ownership or future price gains. — [PapaProxy](https://papaproxy.net/blog/dexscreener-trending.php); [all-dexscreener.pro](https://all-dexcsreener.pro/)
- **Dethective analysis (secondary):** Tracking boosted tokens at set intervals, the analysis found frequent sharp price drops after the boost ended, or even during it. It concluded that DEX Screener, Pump.fun, bots and KOLs consistently profit while retail loses. The original report was not accessible. — [The Holy Coins](https://theholycoins.com/news/dex-screener-boost-report-are-boosted-tokens-a-trap-for-crypto-traders)
- **Vendor anecdote:** A volume-boost seller reports a token rising 250% while trending after paid volume was added. This is a single, commercially motivated anecdote. — [BoostLegends](https://boostlegends.com/blog/how-to-get-trending-on-dexscreener)
- **Boosts as a CTO signal:** CTO-scoring guides treat paid boosts, which cost real SOL, as a sign of team commitment. — [MadeOnSol](https://madeonsol.com/blog/solana-community-takeovers-cto-radar-guide)
- **Monitoring tools:** Apify scrapers and the DEX Screener API expose new, trending and boosted tokens. That makes boost purchases observable as a real-time event feed. — [Apify DEX new token scanner](https://apify.com/gochujang/dex-new-token-scanner)

### Inferences
- Boosts and trending work as *paid attention* that can bring in short-term flow, but sellers widely use the trending ranking as exit liquidity. Trending Score partly responds to volume, which can be wash-traded with "volume bots". For a bot, a boost purchase is better treated as a short-horizon event (possible flow spike) with high dump risk than as a quality signal.
- Getting Trending Score from organic volume is cheap to fake with volume bots. Boosts add a dollar cost, so a boost is slightly harder to fake than raw volume, but it does not indicate quality.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous, independent event study of DEX Screener boost or trending inclusion on price, volume or unique buyers (e.g., abnormal returns at +1h/+6h/+24h after a boost purchase). This is a clear research opportunity that could be built from the DEX Screener boosts API plus on-chain data.
- I could not access the Dethective report's methodology, sample size or numbers.
- I found no data on Token Ads conversion, or on comparable paid placements (Birdeye, DEXTools hot pairs, Photon/GMGN trending) and their effects.
