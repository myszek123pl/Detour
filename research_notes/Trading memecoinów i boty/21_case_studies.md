# Memecoin case studies: winners and failures (as of Nov 2026; snippet-based, ~7 searches)

## Winners: BONK, WIF, POPCAT, GOAT, PNUT, FARTCOIN, MOODENG, SPX6900 (launch, catalysts, peak, drawdown)

### Takeaway
The winners peaked on exchange-listing catalysts or viral narratives and typically retraced hard. GOAT and PNUT hit $1B-$2B market caps within weeks of launch (late 2024); SPX6900 later sat about 85-87% below its July 2025 high. Peak market cap and time-to-peak were only partially recoverable from snippets.

### Inferences
- Tier-1 exchange listing announcements (Coinbase roadmap, Upbit) are repeatable, short-lived catalysts (+12% to +22% on day in cited cases); a bot should treat them as event spikes, not trend starts.
- Peak-to-trough of 60% within a month (GOAT) and 85-87% over 8-9 months (SPX) suggest trailing stops and time-based exits matter more than conviction.
- Old winners can re-rate in sector-wide rotations (May 2025), signalling beta to SOL/market sentiment.
- Rising open interest plus rising large-holder count preceded SPX's 2025 run: a candidate signal.

## Failures and scandals: LIBRA, HAWK TUAH, TRUMP/MELANIA, 2025-2026 rugs

### Takeaway
Celebrity/political launches with insider-controlled supply produced the worst retail losses: LIBRA -94-95% with $87-107M insider extraction, HAWK -87-95% within hours, TRUMP -92-97% and MELANIA -98-99% from peak. Bubblemaps wallet-cluster analysis was the key early-warning/forensic tool.

### Cited Findings
- LIBRA: Milei post, rose to about $4.4-4.56B market cap then fell >94-95%; Bubblemaps tracked insiders pulling about $87M (later $100M; KuCoin cites $107M) from liquidity pools — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/javier-milei-backtracks-4-4b-105824973.html); [Bubblemaps blog](https://blog.bubblemaps.io/how-hayden-davis-rugged-libra-for-100m-with-president-milei/); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/from-4-56b-peak-to-94-crash-milei-s-libra-endorsement-triggers-107m-insider-exit). Nansen: wallets lost $251M — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/307025/some-wallets-still-trading-libra-despite-251-million-losses-nansen). Argentine market dropped >5% on Feb 17, 2025; Milei said he was unaware of details; Hayden Davis (Kelsier) named; Interpol Red Notice requested by a prosecutor — [Decrypt explainer](https://decrypt.co/306987/what-is-libra-the-solana-meme-coin-that-sparked-a-political-scandal). Davis claimed Milei knew of the project (unconfirmed).
- WOLF (Mar 2025): crashed about 99% from $40M peak; Bubblemaps traced creator wallet to Davis-linked wallet (inference, not proof) — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/346504/libra-token-facilitator-hayden-davis-connected-to-40-million-crash-of-wolf-memecoin-bubblemaps).
- HAWK (Dec 4, 2024, Solana): fell 87-91% within 20 min to 3 hours; Bubblemaps said up to about 96% of supply in insider wallets (others cite about 80%); one sniper reportedly sniped 17.5% and made $1.3M; team denied selling — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/hawk-tuah-memecoin-dumps-90-percent-outrage-token-launch-insider); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/hawk-memecoin-launch-fail); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/329558/hawk-tuah-memecoin); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/crypto/2024/12/10/hawk-tuah-memecoin-launch-haliey-welch-pump-dump-rug-pull).
- MELANIA: down 90% within about 3 weeks of launch (Feb 2025) — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-06/melania-memecoin-is-down-90-from-peak-as-novelty-value-wanes); about 98-99% below ATH ($13.05-13.73) by Feb 2026 — [CoinMarketCap Academy](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/melania-token-down-98percent-amid-dollar30m-team-wallet-questions).
- TRUMP: ATH about $75, about 92-95% lower by early 2026 (about $3.6); CryptoRank: retail lost about $4.3B vs insiders about $600M realized (ratio about 20:1); single-sided liquidity on Meteora; remaining insider holdings an overhang — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/trump-meme-coins-took-4-billion-from-retail-investors); [Protos](https://protos.com/trump-and-melanias-coins-are-down-over-95-from-aths/); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/trump-branded-crypto-sinks-92-from-peak-amid-insider-profit-probes/). Other reports give differing loss/insider figures (estimates).
- 2026 incidents: LICK (Jan 2026) -97%, deployer wallet linked to alleged US treasury-seizure theft ($40M) — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/us-treasury-theft-wallet-bundled-memecoin-crashes-97); WhiteWhale -60% (reported $200M to $20M within minutes) after rug accusations — [CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/memecoin-whitewhale-plummet-60-after-rug-pull-accusations/); X "$1M article" winner accused of about $600K from Solana memecoin rugs (Feb 2026) — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-02-11-cry-me-a-river-x-1m-article-winner-accused-of-profiting-600k-from-solana-memecoin-rug-pulls-389385).

### Inferences
- Common red flags preceding collapses: supply concentrated in bundled/related wallets (Bubblemaps cluster), sniper share in first seconds, celebrity/politician-driven launch, single-sided LP that can be withdrawn, deployer wallets tied to previous rugs (Davis/WOLF/LIBRA/MELANIA linkage).
- Collapse timing ranges from minutes (HAWK, WhiteWhale) to weeks (MELANIA, LIBRA within hours of Milei post peak) so pre-trade filters and tight stops are needed; exit liquidity vanishes quickly.
- Insider ratio statistics (about 1:20) suggest late retail entry after a celebrity-driven peak is negative-EV.
