## 1. Total memecoin market cap and DEX volume over time; chain shares

### Takeaway
On CoinGecko's measure, memecoin market cap peaked at about **$150.6B in December 2024** (CoinMarketCap-based reporting gives about $137B on 8 Dec 2024). It fell about 73–77% to roughly **$35–40B by Nov–Dec 2025**. A short rebound took it to about $47–50B in early January 2026, and it has since drifted to about **$30B (Oct 2026)**. Dogecoin alone is about 40–47% of the total. Solana remains the main venue for new-token trading. BNB Chain briefly overtook it in October 2025, but that was a rotation, not a lasting shift.

### Inferences
- Two market-cap baselines circulate: about $150B (CoinGecko) and about $137B (CMC). "Down 70–80% from peak" holds on either. A practical summary is: **about $150B (Dec 2024) → about $35B (Dec 2025) → about $48B (early Jan 2026) → about $30B (Oct 2026)**.
- Market cap is dominated by legacy coins (DOGE, SHIB, PEPE), not new launches. New-launch activity is better tracked through launchpad and DEX volume than through category market cap.
- Chain share for new-token trading is cyclical. Solana and Pump.fun dominated most of 2024–2026, BNB Chain held a brief window in October 2025, and Pump.fun's 2026 move to multiple chains blurs chain-level attribution.

## 2. New tokens launched per day/month and success rates

### Takeaway
Launchpads mint **tens of thousands of tokens a day**. Peaks: about 42k/day on Pump.fun, and **47.8k on Four.meme on 8 Oct 2025**. Pump.fun produced about **11.6M tokens in 2025**. Fewer than 1–2% "graduate" from the bonding curve to a DEX pool: 2025 averaged about 0.6–0.9%, June 2026 was about 0.26%, and Nov 2025–Oct 2026 was about 1.7% (1 in 58). Only about **0.002–0.01%** of launches ever hold a market cap above $1M.

### Cited Findings
**Launch counts**
- Pump.fun once launched **42,000 tokens in 24 hours**, and fewer than 2% will reach a DEX — [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/pumpfun-launched-42000-tokens-in-one-day-fewer-than-2-will-ever-reach-a-dex).
- About **11.6 million tokens** were launched on Pump.fun in calendar 2025 — [Pegasus guide](https://pegasus.botdoctor.dev/guides/pump-fun-graduation-rate).
- On **8 Oct 2025**, Four.meme (BNB Chain) set a record of **47,800 tokens in 24h**, nearly double Pump.fun. It took **83.9% of new launches** between the two platforms that day, after Pump.fun held 93.3% on 1 Oct 2025 — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bnb-chain-memecoin-wave-solana); [Mammoth Times / BreakingCrypto](https://business.mammothtimes.com/mammothtimes/article/breakingcrypto-2025-10-8-bnb-chain-ignites-fourmemes-meteoric-rise-and-czs-meme-coin-szn-declaration-fuel-frenzy).
- As of **Aug 2026**, daily launches "remain in the tens of thousands" — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/pumpfun-daily-revenue-highest-since-september/).
- The Defiant reported that **more than 75% of all memecoins were created in 2024 and 97% are dead** — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/over-75-of-memecoins-were-created-in-2024-but-97-are-dead). edgeX and CoinCentral repeat a "97% failure rate" headline for 2026 — [edgeX](https://pro.edgex.exchange/en-US/news/article/memecoin-statistics-2026-lifespan-returns); [CoinCentral](https://coincentral.com/memecoin-statistics-2026-market-cap-returns-lifespan-data-and-why-97-fail).

**Graduation rates** (bonding curve to DEX; Pump.fun graduation is roughly at a ~$69k–$100k market cap; this threshold is my own background knowledge and was not sourced here)
- **Jul–Aug 2025:** about **0.7–0.8%** of launches — [MEXC news](https://www.mexc.com/news/586607); [Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/pump-fun-token-graduations-six-month-high/).
- **Full-year 2025:** **0.89% of about 11.6M tokens** — [Pegasus](https://pegasus.botdoctor.dev/guides/pump-fun-graduation-rate).
- **Sept 2025 academic sample:** 4,338 graduates, about **0.63%** — [arXiv 2602.14860, "Predicting the success of new crypto-tokens: the Pump.fun case"](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1).
- **Lifetime (Dune dashboards):** about **1.4%**; The Block reports under 2% — [Pegasus summary](https://pegasus.botdoctor.dev/guides/pump-fun-graduation-rate); [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/pumpfun-launched-42000-tokens-in-one-day-fewer-than-2-will-ever-reach-a-dex). Published estimates range from **0.198% to 5%** depending on window and method — [Coinpaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/education/token-graduation-mean-numbers/).
- **May–Jun 2026:** under **0.2%** of launches graduated within 24h (July 2026 preprint). The rate reportedly **fell to 0.26% in June 2026** — [DEXTools](https://www.dextools.io/news/pump-fun-graduation-collapse-solana-fees-2026).
- **Nov 2025–Oct 2026 (Bitquery):** **1 in 58 (about 1.7%)** graduated, and **79% of Pump.fun coins never traded after launch day** — [Bitquery investigation (via search summary; page not fetchable)](https://bitquery.io/investigations/solana-launchpad-year).

**Reaching market-cap thresholds**
- **>$1M:** only **41 of about 1.7M** Pump.fun tokens held a market cap above $1M for several weeks. Protos gives this as 0.002%; BeInCrypto reports 0.0002%, but the correct arithmetic is about 0.0024% — [Protos](https://protos.com/research-finds-less-than-0-002-of-pump-fun-memecoins-succeed). Decrypt counted **89 Pump.fun coins above $1M**, about 0.0045% of nearly 2M tokens, in a 2024 snapshot taken during a lull — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/249107/fewer-than-100-pump-fun-tokens-above-1m-market-cap-amid-meme-coin-lull). About **0.3% of graduated tokens** were above $1M at that time.
- **$1M–$10M:** one analysis found **396 graduated tokens (0.79% of graduates)** in this band — [search summary of Decrypt/Protos-family analyses](https://decrypt.co/249107/fewer-than-100-pump-fun-tokens-above-1m-market-cap-amid-meme-coin-lull).
- **Broad market:** Binance Research found that **only 0.23% of memecoins keep a market cap above $1M** — [Protos](https://protos.com/shock-two-thirds-of-memecoins-now-worthless-says-new-research). Only about **3% of memecoins ever revisit their ATH** — [Cryptoast](https://cryptoast.fr/?p=230337).
- **Trader outcomes:** just **0.4% of Pump.fun traders had made more than $10,000** (Jan 2025) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/300403/pump-fun-traders-millionaires?amp=1).

### Inferences
- An order-of-magnitude funnel for Solana launchpads in 2025–2026: about 20–40k launches/day → about 1% graduate (a few hundred/day) → about 0.01% or less reach and hold $1M → only a handful per month reach $10M+ and very few reach $100M+. The last two steps are extrapolated from the threshold data above, not from a sourced survival curve.
- Graduation rates move with market mood: about 0.26% in the June 2026 slump against about 1.7% averaged over Nov 2025–Oct 2026. Launch counts fall less than quality does.

- No Base, Ethereum or BSC launch-count time series for 2026 was found.
- The Bitquery and CoinLaw pages could not be fetched directly; their figures come from search snippets.

## 3. Major cycle turning points and triggers

### Takeaway
Main sequence: Pump.fun (launched Jan 2024) made launching tokens nearly free. That drove a 2024 boom which peaked in **December 2024** at about $137–150B. The **TRUMP launch (17–19 Jan 2025)** marked peak volume and froth. The **LIBRA scandal (Feb 2025)** and political tokens triggered the bust. Mid-2025 brought a launchpad war (LetsBonk flipped Pump.fun in July 2025) and a BNB Chain rotation (Oct 2025). The **10–11 Oct 2025 liquidation crash** hit the long tail hardest. Late 2025 lows (about $35B) were followed by a short January 2026 rebound and then a grind lower through 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Jan 2025, Pump.fun's peak economics:** cumulative fees reached **$431M at its one-year anniversary (19 Jan 2025)**. The platform made **$14M+ in revenue on 2 Jan 2025** and a **record $15.5M in a day on 24 Jan 2025** — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/337065/pump-fun-set-single-day-revenue-record-raking-in-15-5-million-in-fees-following-anniversary); [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/content/42764560/pump-fun-kicks-off-2025-with-14m-revenue-amid-continued-meme-coin-surge).
- **TRUMP (Jan 2025) as the volume peak:** Solana DEX volume records, Meteora at $33B in January, and Pump.fun's $3.3B weekly ATH were all Trump-driven (see section 1) — [The Block Research](https://www.theblock.co/research/blockchains/layer-by-layer-solana-sets-new-records-with-surging-memecoin-activity-337079); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/solana-memecoin-pump-fun-trump-volume-record).
- **Political tokens as both catalyst and cause of the bust:** CoinGecko's analysis says the political momentum that carried memecoins to $150B also drove the decline — [Cointelegraph (CoinGecko)](https://cointelegraph.com/news/political-tokens-played-key-role-in-memecoin-boom-and-bust-coingecko). It passed **$800M lifetime revenue** in 2025 — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/367585/pump-fun-surpasses-800-million-in-lifetime-revenue).
- **Early Oct 2025, BNB "meme szn":** after CZ promoted BNB memes, Four.meme briefly dominated launches and volume (see sections 1 and 2) — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/pump-fun-rival-form-eyes-40percent-rally-after-cz-declares-bnb-meme-szn). ### Inferences
- Triggers fall into four groups: (a) **supply-side tech**, with near-free launchpads (Pump.fun 2024, PumpSwap March 2025, LetsBonk and Four.meme 2025); (b) **celebrity and political attention spikes** (TRUMP, LIBRA), which mark tops; (c) **macro and leverage shocks** (10 Oct 2025); (d) **chain rotations** driven by ecosystem promotion (CZ and BNB). Rebounds since 2025 have been short and led by legacy coins (PEPE, BONK, FLOKI), not new launches.

- No sourced data on memecoin ETFs (DOGE and others) or US regulatory steps, such as the SEC's February 2025 staff statement that memecoins are not securities. DL News references ETFs and lawsuits but was not read in full.

## 4. Current state of the market (late 2026)

### Takeaway
As of mid-October 2026, memecoins are a **roughly $30B sector, about 80% below the December 2024 peak**, with about $3.4B in daily volume. Dogecoin is about 40% of the total. Volume is falling even while the wider crypto market rises (CoinGecko Q3 2026: memecoin volume -8% against total market cap +39.6%). Launch activity is still very high, but very few tokens survive. Pump.fun remains the dominant launchpad and is profitable: in **August 2026** it posted its best daily revenue (about $2.4M) since September 2025 and 30-day revenue ahead of Hyperliquid. It has expanded to multiple chains and USDC pairs.

### Cited Findings
