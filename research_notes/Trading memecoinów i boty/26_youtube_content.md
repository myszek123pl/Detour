## Strategies taught and consistency with on-chain evidence

### Takeaway
I could not read transcripts, so the specific rules taught are unverified. What I can cite is on-chain data on pump.fun trader profitability, which is the benchmark against which "get rich" promises should be judged: most wallets lose money in most periods, and large profits are concentrated in few wallets.

### Cited Findings
- Dune-based analysis: about 62.5% of pump.fun traders had losses over a six-month window — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/pump-fun-meme-coins-trader-losses/)
- Only 0.4% of pump.fun wallets made more than $10,000 (Dune-based, 2024-25 period) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/300403/pump-fun-traders-millionaires?amp=1)
- CoinGecko wallet-level data (Pump.fun + PumpSwap): profitable wallets were below 50% for most months from April 2024 to late 2025, low of 30.1% in June 2025, then rose to 73.28% in April 2026; wins skewed to small amounts — [CoinGecko Research](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback) (summarized via search result)
- Caveat: realized-PnL datasets miss unrealized holdings and some post-Raydium-migration trades, so one analyst argues the profitable share is understated; other analyses flag bots that consistently profit by frontrunning retail — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-crypto-traders-majority-do-not-realize-profits-dune-data)
- Axiom-focused video summaries frame referral systems as "passive income" and promote finding "100x" trades (summary only; unread) — [DiCloak](https://dicloak.com/video-insights-detail/how-memecoin-millionaires-find-100x-trades-using-axiom)

### Inferences
- "100x" framing conflicts with the data: profits above $10k are rare (0.4%) and the profitable-wallet share varied widely by market phase, suggesting results depend heavily on timing, not only on taught strategy. Treat as inference.
- Typical taught rules (wallet tracking, take-profit ladders, dev/holder checks) could not be confirmed against transcripts.

### Gaps
- No transcripts accessed; strategy list (entry/exit rules, wallet-tracking tips) not sourced. Myth-vs-evidence mapping for specific rules not possible.

### Takeaway
Referral and paid-promo incentives are well documented in the ecosystem; undisclosed paid promotion by crypto influencers was exposed at scale by ZachXBT. I found no source tying a specific memecoin-education YouTuber to a proven scam.

- ZachXBT published a spreadsheet on 200+ influencers with pricing, wallets and on-chain receipts, saying fewer than five labeled posts as ads; tied to memecoin platform Memenetic — [CCN](https://www.ccn.com/news/crypto/zachxbt-leak-crypto-influencers-paid-promotions/); also [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/368956)

## Bot-building tutorials and quality

### Takeaway
Many written and some video tutorials exist for pump.fun sniper bots; best-documented is Chainstack's, with an open repo. Quality varies, and some repos are monetized or ask for private keys.

### Cited Findings
- Chainstack guide: Python bot subscribing via WebSocket to a Solana RPC, watching pump.fun program for new coins, optional auto-sell; embeds a YouTube video "How to research & build a bot (pump.fun Solana example)" — [Chainstack docs](https://docs.chainstack.com/docs/solana-creating-a-pumpfun-bot.md); repo [chainstacklabs/pumpfun-bonkfun-bot](https://github.com/chainstacklabs/pumpfun-bonkfun-bot)
- CoginGecko guide: Python sniper using scan/filter/execute — [CoinGecko Learn](https://www.coingecko.com/learn/how-to-build-pump-fun-sniper-bot-in-python)
- Other tutorials: GetBlock Python sniping guide, GoldRush streaming API series, DEV Community series — [GetBlock](https://docs.getblock.io/guides/blockchain-api-guides/solana-guides/sniping-new-solana-tokens-python-bot-tutorial), [GoldRush](https://goldrush.dev/guides/building-a-solana-memecoin-sniper-bot-using-the-goldrush-streaming-api-part-3/), [DEV](https://dev.to/vietthanhsai/how-to-build-your-solana-sniper-bot-5-f8k)
- Many GitHub "sniper bot" repos exist (e.g., husreo, Frontrunx); some closed-source/monetized, so private-key handling is a risk — [Frontrunx repo](https://github.com/Frontrunx/solana-pumpfunx-sniper-bot)

### Inferences
- Tutorials from infrastructure vendors (Chainstack, GetBlock, GoldRush) have a commercial motive: selling RPC/API access. Reasonable inference, not stated by sources.
- Fake "bot" repos/videos are a known theft vector (see SentinelLABS above).

### Gaps
- No code review of specific YouTube walkthroughs; creator-level quality and views unknown.
