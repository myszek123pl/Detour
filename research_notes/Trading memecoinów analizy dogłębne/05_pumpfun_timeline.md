## Timeline of changes (PumpSwap, creator fees, dynamic fees, PUMP token, acquisitions)

### Takeaway
Core 2025 milestones are sourced: PumpSwap (Mar 2025), creator fees (12 May 2025), Kolscan (10 Jul 2025), PUMP ICO (12 Jul 2025), Dynamic Fees V1 / Project Ascend (Sep 2025), Padre (24 Oct 2025), fee-sharing overhaul (Jan 2026). Nothing sourced for BOOST, Glass Full, livestreams, mobile app, holder rewards or v2/v3 instructions.

### Cited Findings
- Pump.fun started Jan 2024 as a simple token-creation platform. — [Search summary of The Block/Blockworks coverage](https://blockworks.co/news/pumpdotfun-fee-model)
- PumpSwap launched Mar 2025: instant migration, removing the previous 6 SOL migration fee. — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/pumpdotfun-fee-model)
- Creator revenue share launched 12 May 2025: creators earn 0.05% of trading volume; base swap fee 0.25% (0.2% LPs, 0.05% protocol). — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2025-05-13-pumpswap-revenue-tokens-354038)
- Kolscan, first acquisition, announced 10 Jul 2025 just before ICO; tool made free. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/362119/pump-fun-makes-first-acquisition-purchases-solana-based-copy-trading-wallet-tracker-kolscan)
- PUMP ICO 12 Jul 2025: 15% of 1T supply at $0.004; reported $600M raised in 12 min (CoinGape reported a conflicting 33% figure). — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2025-07-12-pump-fun-raises-500-million-in-12-minutes-as-pump-tokens-fully-sell-out-362320)
- Buybacks began within days: 16 Jul, 111,953 SOL (~$18.34M) bought 3.04B PUMP at ~$0.006 avg (per Ember CN); by mid-Aug >$62M spent, 16.5B PUMP absorbed. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/362867/pump-fun-appears-to-initiate-pump-token-buybacks-using-platform-fees-onchain-analysts); [Cointelegraph](https://www.cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-62m-buybacks-pump-token-price)
- PUMP down 42% from ATH (>$0.0068) by 13 Aug 2025; critics cite no governance/staking/revenue share. — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/pump-funs-pump-skyrockets-20-following-buyback-yet-faces-scrutiny-over-utility-concerns/)
- PUMP token erased post-ICO gains within a week amid rising LetsBONK share. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/363462/pump-token-erases-gains-less-than-a-week-after-initial-coin-offering-amid-rising-share-of-letsbonk-memecoin-generator)
- Dynamic Fees V1 (Sep 2025, "Project Ascend"): creator fee % varies with token market cap; tier descriptions differ across outlets. — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/pumpdotfun-fee-model); [DEXTools](https://www.dextools.io/tutorials/what-is-pump-fun-project-ascend-and-the-new-creator-fees)
- Padre (multichain terminal) acquired, announced 24 Oct 2025, price undisclosed; PADRE token fell ~78-80% and lost utility. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pumpfun-acquires-padre-trading-terminal-solana-memecoins)
- Jan 2026 fee overhaul: fees can be split to up to 10 wallets; deployers get only one post-launch change; coincided with highest daily launch count since September. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-01-09-pump-fun-overhauls-creator-fees-amid-launch-spike) (title per search listing: "Pump.fun overhauls creator fees as token launches hit highest daily count since September"; URL: https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-01-09-pump-fun-overhauls-creator-fees-token-launches-highest-daily-september-384975)

### Inferences
- Pre-ICO sequence (Kolscan -> ICO -> buybacks within 4 days) suggests a coordinated token-launch strategy; buybacks funded from fee revenue tie platform activity to PUMP price.
- Creator-fee and fee-sharing changes appear to be repeated levers to re-ignite launch counts.

## Measured effect on volume, launches, graduation

### Takeaway
Monthly DEX volume fell ~81% from Jan 2025 to Jan 2026; fee model changes coincided with fee/launch spikes (Sep 2025, Jan 2026) but not durable volume recovery. Graduation-rate data not found.

### Cited Findings
- PumpSwap April 2025 volume: $11.2B. — [Search summary, Blockworks/The Block coverage](https://blockworks.co/news/pumpdotfun-fee-model)
- Monthly volume >$11.6B Jan 2025 vs ~$2.1B Jan 2026 (-81%, DefiLlama); Feb 2026 ~$1.91B vs $6.1B Feb 2025 (-68%). — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-01-09-pump-fun-overhauls-creator-fees-token-launches-highest-daily-september-384975) (attribution to this outlet unverified; figures came via search summary citing DefiLlama)
- Early Sep 2025 daily fees peaked ~$5.73M, attributed largely to new creator model. — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/pump-price-surges-as-pumpfun-tops-daily-fees)
- New fee model paid ~$2M to creators in first 24h. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/337872/pump-funs-new-fee-model-hands-out-2m-to-creators-in-first-24-hours)
- Daily revenue trended lower in Aug 2025 while buyback price floated 40% above spot. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/366783/pumps-average-token-buyback-price-floats-40-above-spot-market-even-as-daily-revenue-trends-lower)
- Market share surged past Bonk with dynamic fees. — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/pumpdotfun-fee-model)

### Inferences
- Effects of each change are confounded with overall Solana memecoin cycle (Jan 2025 TRUMP-era peak), so attribution is weak; only short-window spikes (creator fee day-1 payouts, Sep fees, Jan 2026 launches) are plausibly causal.
