## 4. Is "AI agents" a tradeable recurring meta for a bot?

### Takeaway
It recurs in bursts (Q4 2024-Jan 2025; Q4 2025-Feb 2026; sub-bursts such as x402 and Clanker/Bankr launcher mania), each ending with 70-97% drawdowns and shrinking fees. It looks tradeable only as short-lived, headline- or launcher-driven momentum with tight exits, not as a hold.

### Cited Findings
- Cycles: Jan 2025 aixbt ATH and the Feb 2025 correction; Jan 2026 peak; Feb 2026 Clanker $8M weekly fees and BNKR ATH on Feb 10, 2026; then slide through Nov 2026 (sources above).
- Launcher/platform migration risk: Bankr left Clanker for Doppler, cutting fees to Clanker (Feb 2026) — [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/token/BNKR)
- Founder/legal risk: ELIZAOS winding down; no buybacks — [The Block](https://theblock.co/news/defi/2026-08-05-eliza-labs-native-token-dead-410774)
- Small-cap agents outperformed in the late-November slide — [CMC](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/ai-news-sector-extends-slide-as-brutal-november-hits-risk-assets)

### Inferences (my own, not sourced)
- Bot approach: trade the narrative's ignition (new launcher, headline, platform fee spike) rather than the sector; use fee/launch-count series (DefiLlama, Dune) as a regime filter, and stay flat when weekly fees are below roughly 10-20% of the peak. The threshold is illustrative, not tested.
- Expect liquidity decay: delistings and thin books make exits costly for the old majors (AIXBT, ELIZAOS); prefer newly launched or Base/Virtuals-native names only while launch counts are rising.
- Backtest on the three observed cycles is a very small sample; treat any edge as unproven.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on bot-relevant dynamics (slippage, holding-period returns, win rates) was found in the six searches; recommend pulling Dune/DefiLlama series directly and fetching primary pages, which I did not do given the search budget.
