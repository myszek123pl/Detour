Method note: 4 web searches done; WebFetch failed (decrypt.co and substack not resolvable in this session), so all facts come from search-result summaries, not full articles. Nearly everything found is 2024-2025; I found NO 2026-specific data on livestreams. Figures are unverified unless stated.

## How the livestream feature works and its timeline

### Takeaway
Streamers launch a coin and stream it on pump.fun; income comes from trading activity in the coin (creator fees), not donations or ads. The feature was shut in Nov 2024 after harmful stunts, reopened in 2025 (phased, 5% of users first), then fully relaunched with a moderation policy.

### Cited Findings
- Streamer revenue depends on trading of their coin rather than donors or ads — [Sportskeeda](https://widgets.sportskeeda.com/us/streamers/how-livestreaming-work-pump-fun)
- Livestreams were suspended in Nov 2024 after stunts, threats against pets and faked suicides — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/352919/creator-capital-markets-pump-fun-streaming-twitch)
- Reopened to 5% of users with stricter moderation (Cointelegraph dated Apr 7, 2025) — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/349647/memecoin-launchpad-pump-fun-reopens-livestreaming-to-5-of-users-with-stricter-moderation-rules); later relaunched to all users — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/pump-fun-reopens-livestreams-new-safeguards/) (sources differ on exact timeline)
- New policy bans violence, harassment, sexual exploitation, illegal activity — [CoinGape](https://coingape.com/brandtalk/pulse/pumpfun-livestream-feature-is-back-but-whats-changed)
- Pump.fun reportedly ~1% of Twitch market share and ~10% of Kick's (unverified, framing from Decrypt/CMC summaries) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/352919/creator-capital-markets-pump-fun-streaming-twitch); related: [CoinMarketCap on Rumble comparison](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/pumpfun-claims-victory-over-rumble-in-streaming)

### Inferences
- Pump.fun is positioning as a streaming platform competitor (Twitch/Kick/Rumble) where the coin replaces the monetization layer.

## Creator-fee economics for streamers

### Takeaway
Sept 2025 Project Ascend dynamic fees (higher fees at low market cap, tapering as cap grows) sharply raised streamer payouts; reports of record ~$20M/week creator earnings. Jan 2026 added fee-sharing tools. Streamer contract terms undisclosed.

### Cited Findings
- Creators earned a record ~$20M in one week, top streamers six figures daily (unverified, aggregator) — [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/b307a-pump-fun-streams-take-off-memecoin-creators-pocket-record-20m-in-a-week); [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/pumpfun-memecoin-streams-explode-as-creators-pocket-record-earnings-in-last-week/)
- Dynamic Fees V1 (Sept 2025): tiered by market cap, lower creator fees as cap grows; one streamer's daily fees went $5.12 to $2,290; $2M paid to creators in first 24h — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/337872/pump-funs-new-fee-model-hands-out-2m-to-creators-in-first-24-hours)
- PumpSwap shares 50% of its trading revenue with creators (from May 2025) — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/354038/pumpswap-revenue-tokens)
- Jan 9, 2026 overhaul: split fees across up to 10 wallets, transfer ownership; Alon Cohen said future iterations will be more market-based — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-01-09-pump-fun-overhauls-creator-fees-token-launches-highest-daily-september-384975)
- Streamers earn most in first days, then fees taper as volume falls — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/352919/creator-capital-markets-pump-fun-streaming-twitch)
- Critics: volume-based rewards incentivize pump-and-dump; tapering fees push streamers to keep market caps low and churn trading — same Decrypt piece
- A KuCoin article forecasting $2B/day average volume in 2026 is promotional; treat as unreliable — [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/how-pump-fun-fee-sharing-reshapes-memecoin-economics-in-2026)

### Inferences
- Streamer incentives favor constant spectacle and volume over holder returns.

## Controversies and moderation

### Takeaway
Nov 2024: users threatened self-harm/violence, fired guns, faked suicides to pump coins; pump.fun disabled livestreaming citing moderation limits. Revived in 2025 with a policy; co-founder said moderation is imperfect.

### Cited Findings
- Threats of self-harm and violence during promo streams; gun-firing recording reported — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/content/42177923/pump-fun-shuts-down-livestream-feature-following-graphic-content-and-threats-of-violence); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/pump-fun-halts-live-streams-as-users-go-to-dangerous-extremes-to-lauch-coins)
- Alon said "hundreds" of streams removed, moderator headcount doubled, automated moderation being built; admitted imperfection — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/328061/pump-fun-live-streams-are-going-too-far-even-for-the-crypto-communitys-liking)
- Gizmodo: shut down citing inability to keep up with moderation — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/pump-fun-shuts-down-its-bonkers-live-streaming-service-2000530104)
- Controversy coincided with record single-day revenue (~$14.4M, unverified) — same search summaries; later revenue reportedly dropped ~66% — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-revenue-dropped-66-percent-backlash)
- NSFW launches also drew backlash — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/pump-fun-disables-livestream-feature-amid-community-backlash-over-nsfw-launches/)

### Gaps
- No 2026 incidents, lawsuits, or regulatory actions found (not searched deeply).

## Trading dynamics: do streamer coins behave differently?

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin: coins pump on attention during streams and fade when attention ends; creator fees front-loaded; no rigorous return statistics found.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
